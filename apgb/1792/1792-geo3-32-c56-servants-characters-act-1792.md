# Servants’ Characters Act 1792

**1792 c.56**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]2 [^F1] or asserting that a servant has been hired for a period of time, or in a station;

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]3 [^F1] or was discharged at any other time, or had not been hired in any previous service; contrary to the fact;
