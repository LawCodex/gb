# Administration of Justice (Language) Act (Ireland) 1737

**1737 c.6**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]I All proceedings in courts of justice, patents, charters, pardons, commissions, &c. shall be in English. and in legible character, not in court-hand,and with usual abbreviations in English, and figures.Penalty £20 to prosecutor.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]II But names of writs, process or technical words, may be expressed as commonly used, so as not in court-hand. Prosecutions to be in three months.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]III . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]V Commissions and proceedings in admiralty may be certified in Latin.
