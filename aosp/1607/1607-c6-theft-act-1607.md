# Theft Act 1607

**1607 c.6**

Act Anent woddis parkis planting dowcattis et cetera

Oure Souerane Lord and Estaittis of this present parliament . . . [^F1] Statutis and Ordinis that quha-soeuir . . . [^F1] Steillis Beis [^F2]... Shall be callit and convenit thairfoir as a braker of the Law . . . [^F1] befoir . . . [^F1] ony . . . [^F1] ordinar magistrat within this realme . . . [^F1] And the penaltie to be Imposit and takin of the Controvenaris befoir the saidis ordinar Inferiour Judges Auchtnocht to exceid the sowme of fourtie pundis of this Realme . . . [^F1]
