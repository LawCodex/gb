# Curators Act 1585 [S]

**1585 c. 25**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Oure Souerane Lord be aduise of this present parliament statutis and ordanis that the nearest agnettis and kinsmen of naturall foulis Ideottis and furious Salbe seruit ressauit and preferrit according to the dispositioun of the commoun law to thair tutorie and curatorie . . . [^F2]
