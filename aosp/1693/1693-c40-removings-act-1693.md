# Removings Act 1693

**1693 c.40**

Act anent the Term of Whitsunday

Our Soveraigne Lord and Lady The King and Queens Majesties with advice and consent of the Estates of Parliament . . . [^F1] Statute and Declare that the Fifteenth day of May . . . [^F1] shall be in all time comeing in place of the former Terme of Whitsunday to all effects whatsoever as well as to Removeings

---

[^F1]: Words repealed by Statute Law Revision (Scotland) Act 1964 (c. 80), Sch. 1
