# March Dykes Act

**1661 c.284**

Act for planting and incloseing of ground

Our soverane Lord . . . [^F1] with consent and advice of his Estates of Parliament . . . [^F1] Statuts and Ordaines that wher inclosours fall to be vpon the border of any persons inheritance the next adiacent heritor shall be at equall paines and charges in building ditching and planting that dyk which parteth their inheritance . . . [^F1]
