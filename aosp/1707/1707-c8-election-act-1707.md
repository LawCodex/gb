# Election Act 1707

**1707 c.8**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

. . . [^F2] It is always hereby expressly Provided and Declared that none shall be capable to . . . [^F3] be Elected for any of the said Estates but such as are Twenty one years of age compleat . . . [^F4]
