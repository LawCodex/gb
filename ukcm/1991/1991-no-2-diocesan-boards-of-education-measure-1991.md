## [^F1]13 Short title, extent and commencement.

[^F1]1 [^F1]This Measure may be cited as the Diocesan Boards of Education Measure 1991.

[^F1]2 [^F1]This Measure shall extend to the whole of the provinces of Canterbury and York, except the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man, but may be applied to the Channel Islands, as defined in the Channel Islands (Church Legislation) Measures 1931 and 1957, or either of them, in accordance with those Measures, and may be extended to the Isle of Man by or under Act of Tynwald.

[^F1]3 [^F1]This Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint.
