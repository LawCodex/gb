# Ecclesiastical Leasing Act 1842

**1842 c. 108**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]1 Ecclesiastical corporations, aggregate or sole, (with certain exceptions,) empowered to grant building leases, under certain restrictions.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]2 Power to reserve increased rent.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]3 Land may be appropriated for streets, yards, &c.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]4 Ecclesiastical corporations may lease running water, and water-leaves and wayleaves.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]5 Power to confirm leases voidable for informality, and to accept surrenders and grant new leases or apportioned leases.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]6 Mining leases may be granted.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]7 Execution of a lease by the necessary consenting parties to be evidence that the requisites of this Act have been complied with.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]8 Act not to restrain existing powers of leasing, except that after a lease under this Act the land shall not be leased except at rack rent.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]9 House of residence, garden, &c. not to be leased.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]10 Improved value of Episcopal Estates to be paid to Commissioners.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]11 Improved value of chapter property above a certain amount to be paid to Commissioners 4 & 5 Vict. c. 39.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]12 Improved value of archdeaconries may be paid to ecclesiastical commissioners.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]14 Portion of improved value under mining leases to be paid to [Church Commissioners].

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]15 Powers of 3 & 4 Vict. c. 113. extended to this Act.

## [^F1]17 Not necessary to surrender under-leases before the grant of a lease under this Act.
