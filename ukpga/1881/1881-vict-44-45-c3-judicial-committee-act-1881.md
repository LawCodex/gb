# Judicial Committee Act 1881

**1881 c.3**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 1 Lords Justices of Appeal to be members of Judicial Committee.

Every person holding or who has held in England the office of a Lord Justice of Appeal shall, if a member of Her Majesty’s Privy Council in England, be a member of the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council [[^F2]but no person shall be a member of that Committee by virtue of this section at any time after the day on which he attains the age of seventy-five years.]

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Judicial Committee Act 1881.
