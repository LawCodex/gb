# Metropolitan Streets Act 1885 (repealed 5.11.1993)

**1885 c.18**

## 1 Short title and construction.

This Act may be cited as the Metropolitan Streets Act, 1885, and shall be construed as one with the [^M1]Metropolitan Streets Act, 1867, and may be cited together with that Act as the Metropolitan Streets Acts, 1867 and 1885.

## 2

In section four of the Metropolitan Streets Act, 1867, “six miles” shall be substituted for “four miles,” and the said Act shall take effect and be construed accordingly.
