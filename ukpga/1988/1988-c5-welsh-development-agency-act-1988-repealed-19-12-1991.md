# Welsh Development Agency Act 1988 (repealed 19.12.1991)

**1988 c. 5**

## 1 Increase of financial limit.

In section 18(3) of the [^M1]Welsh Development Agency Act 1975 (financial limit for the Agency), for the words “£450 million” there shall be substituted the words “ £700 million ”.

## 2 Short title and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Welsh Development Agency Act 1988.

(2) This Act does not extend to Northern Ireland.
