## 16 No matter to be heard, &c. by Judicial Committee unless three members are present exclusive of Lord President.

[^F3]. . . No matter shall be heard, nor shall any order, report, or recommendation be made, by the [^F4]. . . Judicial Committee, in pursuance of [^F4]. . . any [^F4]. . . Act, unless in the presence of at least three members of the said Committee, exclusive of the Lord President of Her Majesty’s Privy Council for the time being.
