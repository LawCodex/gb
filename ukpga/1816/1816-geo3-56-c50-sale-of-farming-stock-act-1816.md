# Sale of Farming Stock Act 1816

**1816 c.50**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]1 [^F1] No sheriff or other officer shall sell or carry off from any lands any straw, chaff, or turnips, in any case, nor any hay or other produce contrary to the covenant.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]2 [^F1] Tenant to give notice of the existence of covenants; and sheriff to give notice to the owner or landlord.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]3 [^F1] Sheriff may dispose of produce subject to an agreement to expend it on the land.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]4 [^F1] Sheriff to assign agreement to landlord.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]5 [^F1] Sheriff to enquire as to the name and residence of the landlord.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]6 [^F1] Landlords not to distrain for rent on purchasers of crops severed from the soil, or other things sold subject to agreement.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]7 [^F1] Sheriff not to sell any clover, &c. growing with corn.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]8 [^F1] Act not to affect contracts.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]9 [^F1] Sheriff not to be liable for damages, unless for wilful omission.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]10 [^F1] Indemnity to sheriff and others acting under the provisions of this Act.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]11 [^F1] Assignee of bankrupt, &c. not to take any crop in any other way than the bankrupt would have been entitled to do.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
