# New Hebrides Act 1980

**1980 c. 16**

An Act to make provision in connection with the attainment by the New Hebrides of independence within the Commonwealth.

[20th March 1980]

## 1 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1]

## 2 Consequential amendments and repeals.

(1) The provisions specified in Schedule 1 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified, being amendments consequential on the attainment of independence within the Commonwealth by the New Hebrides.

(2) The enactments and instruments mentioned in Schedule 2 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

## 3 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F2]

## 4 Short title and commencement.

(1) This Act may be cited as the New Hebrides Act 1980.

(2) Section [^F3]2 and Schedules 1 and 2 shall come into force on the appointed day.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Consequential Amendments

*Section 2(1).*

**1** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F4]

### The Services

**[^F5]2** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Visiting forces

**3** In the [^M1]Visiting Forces (British Commonwealth) Act 1933, section 4 (attachment and mutual powers of command) shall apply in relation to forces raised in the New Hebrides as it applies to forces raised in Dominions within the meaning of the [^M2]Statute of Westminster 1931.

**4** In the [^M3]Visiting Forces Act 1952 in section 1(1)(a) (countries to which the Act applies), at the end there shall be added the words “ the New Hebrides or ”; and, until express provision with respect to the New Hebrides is made by an Order in Council under section 8 of that Act (application to visiting forces of law relating to home forces), any such Order for the time being in force shall be deemed to apply to visiting forces of the New Hebrides.

### Shipping

**[^F6]5** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**6** In the [^M4]Whaling Industry (Regulation) Act 1934, the expression “British ship to which this Act applies” shall not include a British ship registered in the New Hebrides.

### Commonwealth Institute

**[^F7]7** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Offices, shops and railway premises

**8** In section 84(2) of the [^M5]Offices, Shops and Railway Premises Act 1963 (exclusion of visiting forces from Act) before the words “and any country” there shall be inserted the words “ the New Hebrides ”.

**9** In section 78(2) of the [^M6]Office and Shop Premises Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 (exclusion of visiting forces from Act) before the words “and any country” there shall be inserted the words “ the New Hebrides ”.
