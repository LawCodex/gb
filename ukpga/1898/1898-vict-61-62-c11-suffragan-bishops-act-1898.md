# Suffragan Bishops Act 1898

**1898 c.11**

An Act to explain the Act as to Suffragan Bishops.

[1st July 1898]

## 1 Explanation of 26 Hen. 8. c. 14.

Notwithstanding anything contained in the Suffragan Bishops Act 1534 it shall be lawful to nominate, present, and appoint as suffragan bishop a person already consecrated as a bishop, and in that case the letters patent presenting him shall not require his consecration.

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Suffragan Bishops Act 1898 and the [^M1]Suffragan Bishops Act 1534, and the [^M2]Suffragans Nomination Act 1888 and this Act may be cited collectively as the Suffragan Bishops Acts 1534 to 1898.
