# Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1943

**[^F1]1943 c. 16**

[^F1]An Act to amend the law relating to agriculture, agricultural land and the drainage of land, and to amend the Corn Returns Act, 1882.

[^F1][22nd April 1943]

## [^F1]1–17 [^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F2]

## [^F1]18 [^F1] Amendment of Corn Returns Act, 1882.

[^F1]1 [^F1]The [^M1][^F1]Corn Returns Act, 1882, as amended by the [^M2][^F1]Corn Sales Act, 1921, shall have effect subject to the amendments set out in the Third Schedule to this Act, being amendments made for the purpose of —

[^F1]a [^F1]removing the limits as to the number of towns to which the said Act is applicable;

[^F1]b [^F1]abolishing the office of inspector of corn returns; and

[^F1]c [^F1]providing for certain other matters of minor importance.
