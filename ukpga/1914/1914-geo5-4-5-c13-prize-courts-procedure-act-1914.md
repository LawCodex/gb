# Prize Courts (Procedure) Act 1914

**1914 c.13**

An Act to amend the Law relating to Procedure in Prize Courts.

[5th August 1914]

## 1 Procedure in prize courts.

[^X1]1 . . . [^F1] nothing in such repeal shall have the effect of extending section sixteen of that Act to ships of war [[^F2]or military aircraft] taken as prize, [^X2]and accordingly that section shall have effect as if the following words were inserted therein:—“Nothing in this section shall apply to ships of war taken as prize.”

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F3]

## 2 Short title and construction.

This Act may be cited as the [^M1]Prize Courts (Procedure) Act 1914 and shall be construed as one with the Naval Prize Act 1864; and that Act and the [^M2]Prize Courts Act 1894 and this Act may be cited together as the Naval Prize Acts, 1864 to 1914.
