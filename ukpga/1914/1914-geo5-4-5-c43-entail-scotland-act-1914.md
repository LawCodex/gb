# Entail (Scotland) Act 1914

**1914 c.43**

An Act to amend the law of entail in Scotland.

[10th August 1914]

## 1 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Entail (Scotland) Act, 1914.

## 2 Entail Act, 1685, not to apply to future deeds.

[[^F1]No] deed relating to land in Scotland dated after the passing of this Act [[^F1]shall be effective] to entail such land, and no such deed shall be recorded in the register of entails; and any prohibition of alienation, contracting debt, or altering the order of succession, [^F2]... in any such deed shall be null and void:
