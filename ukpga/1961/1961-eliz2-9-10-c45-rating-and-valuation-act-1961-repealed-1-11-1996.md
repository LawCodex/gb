# Rating and Valuation Act 1961 (repealed 1.11.1996)

**1961 c.45**

## Part I — General Provisions as to Valuation and Rating

### 1 [[^F1] Rating of industrial and freight-transport hereditaments.

No reduction in rateable value shall be made under section sixty-eight of the [^M1]Local Government Act, 1929 (which provided for relief from rates in respect of industrial and freight-transport hereditaments) for the purposes of valuation lists coming into force after the passing of this Act.]
