# Naval Pensions Act 1884

**1884 c.44**

An Act to amend the Law respecting the Payment of Naval and Greenwich Hospital Pensions.

[7th August 1884]

## 1 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Naval Pensions Act, 1884.

## [^F1]2 Application to Greenwich Hospital pensions of 28 & 29 Vict. cc. 73, 111, 124.

Any reference in the [^M1]Naval and Marine Pay and Pensions Act, 1865, the [^M2]Navy and Marines (Property of Deceased) Act, 1865 [[^F1]or section 356 of the Armed Forces Act 2006] . . . [^F2], to a pension or a naval pension, or to money payable by the Admiralty, shall be construed to include a naval pension and a Greenwich Hospital pension, gratuity, and allowance, within the meaning of the Greenwich Hospital Acts, 1865 to 1883, and any sum due on account of such a pension, gratuity, or allowance.
