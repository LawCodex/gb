# Agriculture (Miscellaneous War Provisions) (No. 2) Act 1940 (repealed 5.11.1993)

**1940 c. 50**

## 1 Amendments as to drainage of agricultural land.

(1) For subsection (1) of section fifteen of the [^M1]Agriculture (Miscellaneous War Provisions) Act, 1940, there shall be substituted the following subsection—

> (1) Where a scheme for the field drainage of any agricultural land or for the cleansing or other improvement of ditches on any such land or for the supply of water to any such land.—
>
> (a) has been submitted by the owner or occupier of the land to the War Agricultural Executive Committee for the county or county borough in which the land is situated; and
>
> (b) has been approved for the purposes of this section by that Committee;
>
> the Minister may, out of the moneys provided by Parliament, make, towards expenditure incurred by any person in carrying out the scheme, grants of such amounts and subject to such conditions as the Treasury may approve.

## 10 Short title, interpretation and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Agriculture (Miscellaneous War Provisions) (No. 2) Act, 1940.

(2) In this Act the expression “the Minister” means [[^F9]the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food] and the expression “Defence Regulations” means Regulations made under the [^M3]Emergency Powers (Defence) Acts, 1939 and 1940.

(3) Sections one to three of this Act shall not extend to Scotland.

(4) This Act, . . . [^F10] shall not extend to Northern Ireland.
