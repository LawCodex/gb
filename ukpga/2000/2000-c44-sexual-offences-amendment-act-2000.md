# Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 2000

**2000 c. 44**

An Act to reduce the age at which, and to make provision with respect to the circumstances in which, certain sexual acts are lawful; to make it an offence for a person aged 18 or over to engage in sexual activity with or directed towards a person under that age if he is in a position of trust in relation to that person; and for connected purposes.[^F1]

[30th November 2000]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Commons in this present Parliament assembled, in accordance with the provisions of the Parliament Acts 1911 and 1949, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 3 Abuse of position of trust.

[^F6]1 [^F6]Subject to subsections (2) and (3) below, it shall be an offence for a person aged 18 or over—

[^F6]a [^F6]to have sexual intercourse (whether vaginal or anal) with a person under that age; or

[^F6]b [^F6]to engage in any other sexual activity with or directed towards such a person,

[^F6]if (in either case) he is in a position of trust in relation to that person.

[^F6]2 [^F6] Where a person (“ [^F6]A[^F6] ”) is charged with an offence under this section of having sexual intercourse with, or engaging in any other sexual activity with or directed towards, another person (“ [^F6]B[^F6] ”), it shall be a defence for A to prove that, at the time of the intercourse or activity—

[^F6]a [^F6]he did not know, and could not reasonably have been expected to know, that B was under 18;

[^F6]b [^F6]he did not know, and could not reasonably have been expected to know, that B was a person in relation to whom he was in a position of trust; or

[^F6]c [^F6]he was lawfully married to[^F6][[^F7], or in civil partnership with,][^F6] B.

[^F6]3 [^F6] It shall not be an offence under this section for a person (“ [^F6]A[^F6] ”) to have sexual intercourse with, or engage in any other sexual activity with or directed towards, another person (“ [^F6]B[^F6] ”) if immediately before the commencement of this Act—

[^F6]a [^F6]A was in a position of trust in relation to B; and

[^F6]b [^F6]a sexual relationship existed between them.

[^F6]4 [^F6]A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

[^F6]a [^F6]on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or to both;

[^F6]b [^F6]on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or to a fine, or to both.

[^F6]5 [^F6] In this section, “ [^F6]sexual activity[^F6] ”—

[^F6]a [^F6]does not include any activity which a reasonable person would regard as sexual only with knowledge of the intentions, motives or feelings of the parties; but

[^F6]b [^F6]subject to that, means any activity which such a person would regard as sexual in all the circumstances.

## 4 Meaning of “position of trust”.

[^F6]1 [^F6] For the purposes of section 3 above, a person aged 18 or over (“ [^F6]A[^F6] ”) is in a position of trust in relation to a person under that age (“ [^F6]B[^F6] ”) if any of the four conditions set out below, or any condition specified in an order made by the Secretary of State by statutory instrument, is fulfilled.

[^F6]2 [^F6]The first condition is that A looks after persons under 18 who are detained in an institution by virtue of an order of a court or under an enactment, and B is so detained in that institution.

[^F6]3 [^F6]The second condition is that A looks after persons under 18 who are resident in a home or other place in which—

[^F6]a [^F6] accommodation and maintenance are provided by an authority under section 23(2) of the [^M4][^F6] Children Act 1989 or Article 27(2) of the [^M5][^F6] Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995;

[^F6]b [^F6]accommodation is provided by a voluntary organisation under section 59(1) of that Act or Article 75(1) of that Order; or

[^F6]c [^F6] accommodation is provided by an authority under section 26(1) of the [^M6][^F6] Children (Scotland) Act 1995,

[^F6]and B is resident, and is so provided with accommodation and maintenance or accommodation, in that place.

[^F6]4 [^F6]The third condition is that A looks after persons under 18 who are accommodated and cared for in an institution which is—

[^F6]a [^F6]a hospital;

[^F6]b [^F6]a [^F6][[^F8]residential care home, nursing home, mental nursing home or][^F6] private hospital;

[^F6][[^F9] bb [^F6]provided by a care home service;]

[^F6]c [^F6]a community home, voluntary home, children’s home or residential establishment; or

[^F6]d [^F6] a home provided under section 82(5) of the [^M7][^F6] Children Act 1989,

[^F6]and B is accommodated and cared for in that institution.

[^F6]5 [^F6]The fourth condition is that A looks after persons under 18 who are receiving full-time education at an educational institution, and B is receiving such education at that institution.

[^F6]6 [^F6]No order shall be made under subsection (1) above unless a draft of the order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

[^F6]7 [^F6]A person looks after persons under 18 for the purposes of this section if he is regularly involved in caring for, training, supervising or being in sole charge of such persons.

[^F6]8 [^F6]For the purposes of this section a person receives full-time education at an educational institution if—

[^F6]a [^F6]he is registered or otherwise enrolled as a full-time pupil or student at the institution; or

[^F6]b [^F6]he receives education at the institution under arrangements with another educational institution at which he is so registered or otherwise enrolled.

## 7 Short title, construction, commencement and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 2000.

(2) For the purposes of the [^M26]Scotland Act 1998, this Act shall be taken to be a pre-commencement enactment within the meaning of that Act.

(3) This Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint; and different days may be appointed for different purposes.

(4) This Act extends to Northern Ireland.
