# Race Relations (Amendment) Act 2000

**2000 c. 34**

An Act to extend further the application of the Race Relations Act 1976 to the police and other public authorities; to amend the exemption under that Act for acts done for the purpose of safeguarding national security; and for connected purposes.

[30th November 2000]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

### 10 Short title, commencement and extent.

[^F3]1 [^F3]This Act may be cited as the Race Relations (Amendment) Act 2000.

[^F3]2 [^F3]Sections 1 to 9 (including Schedules 1 to 3) shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint; and different days may be appointed for different purposes.

[^F3]3 [^F3]An order under this section—

[^F3]a [^F3]shall be made by statutory instrument; and

[^F3]b [^F3]may make such transitory, transitional or saving provision as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

[^F3]4 [^F3]Transitory provision made in exercise of the power conferred by subsection (3)(b) may, in particular, include provision made in consequence of any provision of any other Act passed before, or in the same session as, this Act not having come into force.

[^F3]5 [^F3]No amendment by this Act of an enactment shall be taken, for the purposes of the [^M1][^F3]Scotland Act 1998, to be a pre-commencement enactment within the meaning of that Act unless the amendment so provides.

[^F3]6 [^F3]Any amendment or repeal by this Act of an enactment has the same extent as the enactment amended or repealed.
