# Provisional Order (Marriages) Act 1905

**1905 c.23**

An Act to enable Provisional Orders to be made for removing any invalidity or doubt attaching to Marriages by reason of some informality.

[11th August 1905]

## 1 †Provisional orders for removing doubts as to validity of marriages.

(1) [[^F1]Lord Chancellor] may, in the case of marriages solemnised in England which appear to him to be invalid or of doubtful validity by reason of some informality, make [[^F2]an order] for the purpose of removing the invalidity or doubt.

(2) The draft of every such order shall be advertised, in such manner as the [[^F3]Lord Chancellor] thinks fit, not less than one month before the order is made, and the [[^F3]Lord Chancellor] shall consider all objections to the order sent to him in writing during that month, and shall, if it appears to him necessary, direct a local inquiry into the validity of any such objections.

[[^F4](3) An order of the [[^F3]Lord Chancellor] under this Act shall be subject to special parliamentary procedure.]

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Provisional Order (Marriages) Act, 1905.
