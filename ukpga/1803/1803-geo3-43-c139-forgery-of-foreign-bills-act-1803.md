# Forgery of Foreign Bills Act 1803

**1803 c. 139**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]1 [^F1] Persons forging, &c., foreign bills of exchange, &c. or tendering the same in payment, guilty of felony, and liable to be transported.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]2 [^F1] Persons engraving plates for foreign bills of exchange, &c., or printing them, without lawful authority, to be deemed guilty of a misdemeanour. Act not to alter any law in force for the punishment of forgery.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
