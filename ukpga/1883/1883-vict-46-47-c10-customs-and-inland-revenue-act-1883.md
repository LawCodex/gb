# Customs and Inland Revenue Act 1883

**1883 c.10**

[^F1]An Act to grant certain Duties of Customs and Inland Revenue, to alter other Duties, and to amend the Laws relating to Customs and Inland Revenue.

[31st May 1883]

## 1 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Customs and Inland Revenue Act, 1883.

### 4 Alteration of date of expiration of certain game licences.

Every licence or certificate to kill game now in force, or to be taken out under the [^M1]Game Licences Act, 1860, which expires, or would expire, under the provisions of that Act on the fifth day of April, shall expire on the thirty-first day of July, and wherever in such provisions the fifth day of April is mentioned or referred to, the same shall be read as if the thirty-first day of July had been therein inserted in lieu of the said fifth day of April.

### 5 Game licences for short periods.

A licence or certificate to kill game may be taken out under the provisions of the Game Licences Act, 1860, for a continuous period of fourteen days to be specified in such licence or certificate, and there shall be granted and paid thereon the duty of [[^F4]two pounds.]
