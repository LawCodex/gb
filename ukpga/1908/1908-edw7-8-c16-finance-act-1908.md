# Finance Act 1908

**1908 c.16**

[^F1]An Act to grant certain duties of Customs and Inland Revenue, to alter other duties, and to amend the Law relating to Customs and Inland Revenue and the National Debt, and to make other provisions for the financial arrangements of the year.

## Part III — Local Taxation Licences

### 6 †Collection of duties on certain local taxation licences by county councils.

(1) The power to levy the duties on local taxation licences to which this section applies shall, [[^F6]in England [^F7]. . . be vested in the councils of districts][[^F8]and in Wales be vested in the councils of counties and county boroughs].

(2) His Majesty may, by Order in Council, . . . [^F9] make provision for the furnishing by [[^F10][^F11]. . . councils] of returns to the Local Government Board as to the amounts levied under the power transferred by this section.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F9]

Subsections (3) (4) and (5) of section twenty of the [^M1]Local Government Act, 1888, and any other provisions of that Act relating to the levy of the duties on local taxation licences by county councils shall, as respects the duties to which this section applies, have effect as if the power to levy those duties had been transferred under subsection (3) of section twenty of that Act [[^F12]and as if any reference in those provisions to a county council were a reference to a council having power to levy those duties after 1st April 1974][[^F13]or, in relation to Wales, to a council having power to levy those duties after 1st April 1996]
