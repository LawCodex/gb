# Circuit Courts (Scotland) Act 1828

**1828 c.29**

An Act to authorize additional Circuit Courts of Justiciary to be held, and to facilitate Criminal Trials in Scotland.

[19th June 1828]

## 26 Provisions of 43 Geo. 3. c. 141. extended to all inferior judges.

The provisions of the [^M1]Justices Protection Act 1803 shall extend to all inferior judges and magistrates in Scotland, in regard to any sentence pronounced or proceeding had in any criminal trial.
