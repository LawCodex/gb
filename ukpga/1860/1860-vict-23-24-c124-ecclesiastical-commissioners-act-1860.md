## 14 Preference may be given to places where contribution is made in aid of grant.

In making additional provision for the cure of souls under section sixty-seven of the [^M1]Ecclesiastical Commissioners Act 1840, preference may be given, if the said Commissioners see fit, to those places in respect of which contributions from other sources are made in aid of grants out of the common fund[^F5]...

## 15 In mining districts Commissioners may make grants for cure of souls.

In districts in which large masses of population are collected for the purpose of working mines, it shall be lawful for the said Commissioners, by resolution of a general meeting, from time to time to make grants to meet benefactions for the purpose of making temporary provision for the cure of souls.
