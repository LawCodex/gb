# Registration of Leases (Scotland) Amendment Act 1877

**1877 c.36**

An Act to amend the Registration of Leases (Scotland) Act, 1857.

[6th August 1877]

## 1 “Commissary” to be read in recited Act after “Sheriff”.

Section nineteen of the said recited Act shall be read as if the word “ Commissary” occurred therein immediately after the word “sheriff”.

## 2 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1]

## 3 Short title.

This Act may be cited for all purposes as the Registration of Leases (Scotland) Amendment Act, 1877.
