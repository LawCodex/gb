# Customs, Inland Revenue, and Savings Banks Act 1877

**1877 c. 13**

An Act to grant certain Duties of Customs and Inland Revenue, and to amend the Laws relating to Customs, Inland Revenue, and Savings Banks.

[11th June 1877]

## 1 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Customs, Inland Revenue, and Savings Banks Act 1877.

## PART IV — Stamps

### 12 Transmission and custody of inventories in Scotland.

[[^F8]To the extent that the Registrar, Capital Taxes Office at Edinburgh may require,] inventories of the personal or movable estate and effects of deceased persons which shall be exhibited and recorded in Scotland, under the provisions of any Act of Parliament, shall, together with the oath or affirmation relating thereto, be transmitted by the commissary clerks or the sheriff clerks . . . [^F9] to the [[^F10]Registrar, Capital Taxes Office at] Edinburgh . . . [^F11]; and all enactments relating to any such inventories shall be read as if the officer to or with whom inventories are thereby directed to be transmitted or lodged were the [[^F12]Registrar, Capital Taxes Office at] Edinburgh.
