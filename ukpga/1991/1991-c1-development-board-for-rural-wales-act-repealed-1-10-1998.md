# Development Board for Rural Wales Act (Repealed 1.10.1998)

**1991 c. 1**

## 1 Increase of financial limit.

In section 12 of the [^M1]Development of Rural Wales Act 1976 (financial limit for the Development Board for Rural Wales), for subsection (2) there shall be substituted—

> (2) The said limit is £175 million.

;

and section 2(3) of the [^M2]Industry Act 1981 (which increased the previous limit to £100 million) is hereby repealed.

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Development Board for Rural Wales Act 1991.
