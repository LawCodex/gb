# Sale of Offices Act 1809

**1809 c.126**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]1 [^F1] Provisions of 5 & 6 Ed. 6. c. 16 extended to other offices.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]2 [^F1] When right of appointment forfeited, to go to his Majesty.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]3 [^F1] Persons buying or selling offices, or receiving or paying money or rewards for offices, guilty of a misdemeanor.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]4 [^F1] Persons receiving or paying money for soliciting offices, and any negociations and pretended negociations relating thereto, guilty of a misdemeanor.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]5 [^F1] Persons opening or advertising houses for transacting business relating to the sale of offices, guilty of a misdemeanor.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]6 [^F1] Inflicting a penalty on persons advertising or publishing the names of brokers or agents.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]7 [^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]9 [^F1] Act not to extend to offices excepted in former Act, nor to securities or transactions under legal securities.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]10 [^F1] Act not to extend to lawful deputations where payment of principal or deputy is out of the fees.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]11 [^F1] Act not to extend to annual payments out of the fees of any office, to any person formerly holding such office.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]12 [^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]13 [^F1] Manner of punishing offenders in Scotland.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]14 [^F1] Offences committed abroad shall be tried in King’s Bench.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
