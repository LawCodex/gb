# Edinburgh University (Transfer of Patronage) Act 1897

**1897 c.13**

An Act for transferring the right of Presentation to the Professorships of Botany and Natural History in the University of Edinburgh.

[3rd June 1897]

## 1 Transfer of presentation to professorships.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1] The right of presentation to the Professorship of Natural History in the University of Edinburgh. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1] shall. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1] be exercised by the curators of the said University appointed under the provisions of section thirteen of the [^M1]Universities (Scotland) Act 1858; and the right of presentation to the Professorship of Botany in the said University. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1] shall. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1] be exercised by Her Majesty, Her heirs and successors, in right of Her Crown.

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Edinburgh University (Transfer of Patronage) Act 1897.
