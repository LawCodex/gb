# Removal Terms (Scotland) Act 1886 Amendment Act 1890

**1890 c.36**

An Act to amend the Removal Terms (Scotland) Act 1886.

[4th August 1890]

## 1 Short title, and extent of Act.

This Act may be cited as the Removal Terms (Scotland) Act 1886 Amendment Act 1890, and shall extend to Scotland only.

## 2 Terms as to service and removals.

Where under any contract of service . . . [^F1]the term for the commencement or the expiry of such service shall be one or other of the terms of Whit Sunday or Martinmas, a servant hired by the half-year or year shall, in the absence of express stipulation to the contrary, enter upon or quit such service (any custom or usage to the contrary notwithstanding) at noon on the twenty-eighth day of May if the term be Whit Sunday, or at noon on the twenty-eighth day of November if the term be Martinmas, or on the following day at the same hour when the said terms or either of them fall on a Sunday.

Notwithstanding anything in this Act contained, in all cases in which warning of removal from a house is required forty days before a Whit Sunday or Martinmas term of removal, such warning shall be given forty days before the fifteenth day of May and the eleventh day of November respectively.
