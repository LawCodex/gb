# Married Women’s Property Act 1907

**1907 c.18**

An Act to amend the Married Women’s Property Act, 1882.

[21st August 1907]

## 1 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1]

## 2 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F2]

## 3 Married woman entitled to prior estate to be protector of settlement alone.

(1) Where a married woman would, if single, be the protector of a settlement in respect of a prior estate, . . . [^F3] then she alone shall, in respect of that estate, be the protector of the settlement.

(2) This section applies to disentailing assurances and surrenders made after the thirty-first day of December one thousand eight hundred and eighty-two, and as well before as after the commencement of this Act.

## 4 †Short title; commencement; construction.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Married Women’s Property Act, 1907.

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F4]

(3) This Act shall not extend to Scotland.

(4) This Act shall be construed with the Married Women’s Property Acts, [^M1]1882 [^M2], 1884, and [^M3]1893, and those Acts and this Act may be cited together as the Married Women’s Property Acts, 1882 to 1907.
