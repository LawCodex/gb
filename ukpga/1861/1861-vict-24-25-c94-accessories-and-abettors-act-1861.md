# Accessories and Abettors Act 1861

**1861 c.94**

An Act to consolidate and amend the Statute Law of England and Ireland relating to Accessories to and Abettors of indictable Offences.

[6th August 1861]

## 1 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1]

## As to abettors in misdemeanors:

### 8 Abettors in misdemeanors.

Whosoever shall aid, abet, counsel, or procure the commission of [[^F2]any indictable offence] , whether the same be [[^F2]an offence] at common law or by virtue of any Act passed or to be passed, shall be liable to be tried, indicted, and punished as a principal offender.
