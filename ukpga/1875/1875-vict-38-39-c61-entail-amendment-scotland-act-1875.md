## [^F1]5 †Amendment of section 3 of 11 & 12 Vict. c. 36. Consents to disentail estates entailed before 1st August 1848, may be given in course of application, and when such consents are not given, the court may assess the value of the heirs interests and dispense with such consents, on payment or security of such value. In case of nearest heir consent must be given by himself.
