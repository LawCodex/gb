# Statute Law Revision (Substituted Enactments) Act 1876 (repealed 5.11.1993)

**1876 c.20**

## 1 Substitution of Summary Jurisdiction Act for repealed Act 7 & 8 Geo. 4. c. 30. in certain sections of the Inclosure Acts.

. . . [^F1] Any offence under section ten of the [^M1]Inclosure Act 1848 and under section ten of the [^M2]Inclosure Act 1849 and under section thirty-three of the [^M3]Inclosure Act 1852 shall be deemed to be an offence punishable on summary conviction under the Summary Jurisdiction . . . [^F2] Acts . . . [^F2]: Provided . . . [^F3] that any information in relation to any such offence as is mentioned in this section shall be heard, tried, determined, and adjudged before two justices.
