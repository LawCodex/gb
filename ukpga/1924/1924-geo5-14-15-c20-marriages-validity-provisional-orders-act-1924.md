# Marriages Validity (Provisional Orders) Act 1924

**1924 c.20**

An Act to amend the Provisional Order (Marriages) Act, 1905.

[14th July 1924]

## 1 Amendment of s. 1 of 5 Edw. 7. c. 23.

[[^F1]An order] made by a Secretary of State under section one of the [^M1]Provisional Order (Marriages) Act, 1905, for the purpose of removing the invalidity or doubt as to the validity of any marriages, may include such supplemental, incidental and consequential provisions, including provisions for relieving from liability ministers who may have solemnised the marriages to which the order relates, as appear to him to be necessary or expedient.

## 2 Short title and citation.

This Act may be cited as the Marriages Validity (Provisional Orders) Act, 1924, and the Provisional Order (Marriages) Act, 1905, and this Act may be cited together as the Marriages Validity (Provisional Orders) Acts, 1905 and 1924.
