. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]13 Court of Session to direct purchase money to be applied to payment of debts, &c.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]14 By whom the costs of parties interested and appearing shall be paid.
