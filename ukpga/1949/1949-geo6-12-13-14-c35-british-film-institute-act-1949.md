# British Film Institute Act 1949

**1949 c.35**

An Act to provide for the payment to the British Film Institute of grants out of moneys provided by Parliament.

[31st May 1949]

## 1 Payment of grants to British Film Institute.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1]the Treasury may from time to time make grants to that Institute of such amounts as they think fit out of moneys provided by Parliament.

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the British Film Institute Act 1949.
