# Royal And Parliamentary Titles Act 1927

**1927 c.4**

An Act to provide for the alteration of the Royal Style and Titles and of the Style of Parliament and for purposes incidental thereto.

[12th April 1927]

## 1 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^F1]

## 2 Alteration of the style of Parliament.

(1) Parliament shall hereafter be known as and styled the Parliament of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland; . . . [^F2]

(2) In every . . . [^F3] public document issued after the passing of this Act the expression “United Kingdom” shall, unless the context otherwise requires, mean Great Britain and Northern Ireland.

## 3 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Royal and Parliamentary Titles Act 1927.
