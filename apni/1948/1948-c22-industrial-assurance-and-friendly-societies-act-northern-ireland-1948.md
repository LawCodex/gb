# Industrial Assurance and Friendly Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1948

**1948 Chapter 22**

An Act to amend the Friendly Societies Acts (Northern Ireland), 1896 to 1929, and the Industrial Assurance Acts (Northern Ireland), 1924 to 1929, and to amend provisions corresponding or relating to provisions of those Acts contained in the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts (Northern Ireland), 1893 to 1929, and other enactments, as to payments on deaths of children, payments on deaths where no grant of probate or administration has been made, the designation of auditors appointed thereunder, the mode of determination of disputes and interpretation.

[23rd December 1948]

## Amendments as to Insurances Authorised, and as to Alienation of Insurance Benefits

*Ss.1‐5 rep. by SLR (NI) 1954; 1966 c.18; 1970 c.31 (NI); 1979 NI 13*

### 6 Prohibition of insuring money to be paid on death of a child under ten.

(1) …[^F1] a trade union ([^F2]...), shall not insure so as to render any sum payable under the insurance on the death of any person at any time before he or she attains the age of ten years, otherwise than by way of repayment of the whole or any part of premiums paid:

*Proviso rep. by 1992 NI 5*

*Subs. (2) rep. by 1992 NI 5*

*S. 7 rep. by 1992 NI 5*
