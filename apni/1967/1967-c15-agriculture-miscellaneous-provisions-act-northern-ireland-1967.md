# Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1967

**1967 Chapter 15**

An Act to amend the law with respect to the valuation of agricultural property for the purposes of rates; to amend or repeal certain enactments relating to live stock breeding, agricultural workers' wages and holidays, scutch mills and flax, the production and sale of milk and milk products, the marketing of agricultural produce and the drainage of land; and for purposes connected with certain of the matters aforesaid.

[27th June 1967]

### 19 Repeals and savings.

*Subs.(1), with Schedule 2, effects repeals*

(2) The repeal, taking effect by virtue of subsection (1), of any enactment mentioned in Part I of Schedule 2 shall not affect the application of that enactment in relation to holidays of any description during any period falling between the date of such repeal and the date of the commencement of the first order made by the Agricultural Wages Board for Northern Ireland with respect to holidays of that description, but during any such period that enactment, in relation to such holidays, shall be deemed to continue in force and shall have effect in all respects as if this Act had not been passed.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2), holidays such as are described in each of the following paragraphs of the Schedule to the Agricultural Workers' Holidays Act (Northern Ireland) 1956 [^M1], that is to say, paragraph 1, paragraph 2 and paragraph 6, shall be treated as holidays of the same description.

### 20 Short title and commencement.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1967.
