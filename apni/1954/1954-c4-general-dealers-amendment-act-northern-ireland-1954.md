# General Dealers (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1954

**1954 Chapter 4**

An Act to exclude from the operation of the General Dealers (Ireland) Act, 1903, transactions respecting iron.

[9th March 1954]

## 1 Amendment of 1903 c.44.

The General Dealers (Ireland) Act, 1903, shall cease to have effect in relation to transactions respecting iron, …[^F1]

## 2 Short title, and citation.

(1) This Act may be cited as the General Dealers (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland), 1954

(2) This Act and the General Dealers (Ireland) Act, 1903, may be cited together as the General Dealers Acts (Northern Ireland), 1903 and 1954.
