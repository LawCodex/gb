# Statute Law Revision Act (Northern Ireland) 1954

**1954 Chapter 35**

An Act to repeal certain enactments which have ceased to be in force or have become unnecessary; and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

[21st December 1954]

*S. 1, with Schedule, effects repeals with savings*

## 2 Short title, construction and citation.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Statute Law Revision Act (Northern Ireland), 1954.

(2) This Act shall be construed as one with the Statute Law Revision Acts (Northern Ireland), 1952 and 1953, and those Acts and this Act may be cited together as the Statute Law Revision Acts (Northern Ireland), 1952 to 1954.
