# Rules Publication Act (Northern Ireland) 1925

**1925 Chapter 6**

An Act to amend, in its application to Northern Ireland, the Law relating to the Publication of Statutory Rules and to the laying of Statutory Rules before Parliament.

[9th June 1925]

*The Act has been repealed subject to a saving for section 3 (application of Documentary Evidence Acts) in relation to any statutory rule within the meaning of the Act (including any rule which was deemed to be such a rule by virtue of section 4(1) of the Northern Ireland Act 1955) made before 1.1.1959 (see 1979 NI 12 art.11(3)).*
