# Agriculture (Abolition of County Committees) Act (Northern Ireland) 1972

**1972 Chapter 15**

An Act to provide for the abolition of county committees of agriculture established under the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949, and for purposes connected therewith.

[28th March 1972]

## 1 Abolition of county committees of agriculture.

(1) The Minister of Agriculture may, by order made subject to affirmative resolution,—

(a) abolish, as from such date[^F1] as may be specified in the order, the county committees of agriculture established under section 2 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949 [^M1];

(b) transfer, to such persons as may be specified in the order, the functions exercisable immediately before that date by those committees under section 3 of that Act; and

(c) make such other provisions as appear to him to be necessary or expedient in consequence of such abolition.

*Subs.(2) repeals ss.2, 3 of, and sch.1 to, 1949 c.2 (NI); s.2(4) of 1959 c.2 (NI)*

## 2 Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Agriculture (Abolition of County Committees) Act (Northern Ireland) 1972.
