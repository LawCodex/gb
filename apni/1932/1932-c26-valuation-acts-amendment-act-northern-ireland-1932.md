# Valuation Acts Amendment Act (Northern Ireland) 1932

**1932 Chapter 26**

An Act to make provision for the periodical revaluation of rateable property in Northern Ireland, and otherwise to amend the law relating to the valuation of such property.

[18th February 1932]

*The Act has been repealed but the repeal of s.7 (Abolition of assessment on half rents) is subject to a saving for payments or allowances relating to half-rents which a landlord is required to make (see 1972 NI 16 art.63(2)(e) schs.18,19)*
