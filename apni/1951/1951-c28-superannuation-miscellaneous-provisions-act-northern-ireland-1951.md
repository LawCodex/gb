# Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1951

**1951 Chapter 28**

An Act to amend the law relating to the superannuation of teachers and the law relating to pensions and other similar payments to be made to and in respect of persons who have been in certain employment; to make further provision with respect to the superannuation of civil servants; and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

[20th December 1951]

*Part I (ss. 1‐4) rep. with saving by 1972 NI 10*

*Part II (ss. 5‐11) rep. by 1954 c. 33 (NI); 1966 c. 27 (NI); 1967 c. 24 (NI)*

*Part III—S. 12(1) rep. by 1972 NI 10; subs. (2) inserts s. 5B in 1950 c. 10 (NI); subs. (3) rep. by 1972 NI 10*

## PART IV — General

*S. 13 rep. by 1972 NI 10*

### 14 Short title.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland), 1951.
