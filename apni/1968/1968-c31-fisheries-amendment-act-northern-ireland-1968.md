# Fisheries (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1968

**1968 Chapter 31**

An Act to amend the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 and the Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952.

[12th December 1968]

## 1 Fishing licences.

(1) …[^F1], the provisions set out in Schedule 1 shall be substituted for the provisions of Part III of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^M1] (in this Act referred to as “the Act of 1966” ), …[^F1].

*Subs. (2) rep. by SLR 1980*

*S. 2 inserts s. 15A in 1966 c. 17 (NI)*

*S. 3 rep. by 1973 NI 1*

*S. 4 inserts s. 185A in 1966 c. 17 (NI)*

*S. 5(1) rep. by SLR 1976; subs. (2)‐(11) amend ss. 54, 56, 58, 59, 62, 111, 112, 120, 176, 195 of 1966 c. 17 (NI)*

*S. 6 amends ss. 104, 197, 198 of 1966 c. 17 (NI)*

*S. 7, with Schedule 2 effects amendments*

*S. 8 amends ss. 42, 48, 51A, 58, 63 of 1952 c. 5 (NI)*

## 9 Short title and citation.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1968, and may be cited—

(a) together with the Act of 1966 as the Fisheries Acts (Northern Ireland) 1966 and 1968;

(b) together with the Foyle Fisheries Acts (Northern Ireland) 1952 and 1962 as the Foyle Fisheries Acts (Northern Ireland) 1952 to 1968.
