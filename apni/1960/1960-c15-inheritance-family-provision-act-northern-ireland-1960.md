# Inheritance (Family Provision) Act (Northern Ireland) 1960

**1960 Chapter 15**

An Act to amend the law relating to dispositions of estates of deceased persons; and for purposes connected therewith.

[20th December 1960]

*The Act has been repealed subject to savings for—*

(a) *applications made thereunder for financial provision out of the estate of any person who died before 1.9.1979; and*

(b) *orders made and directions given thereunder which were in force at 1.9.1979*

*(see 1979 NI 8 art.28 sch.)*
