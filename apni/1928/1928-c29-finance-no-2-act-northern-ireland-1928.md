# Finance (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 1928

**1928 Chapter 29**

An Act to grant a stamp duty upon certain bankers' licences, and to amend the law relating to stamp duty and a duty of Excise.

[18th December 1928]

*The Act ceased to have effect as from 1st August 1970, except in so far as the Schedule is required for the purposes of the definition of licensed bank in section 22(1) of the Prevention of Fraud (Investments) Act (NI) 1940 (see s.7(2) Finance Act (NI) 1970)*

## SCHEDULE

The Bank of Ireland

The Provincial Bank of Ireland, Ltd.

The National Bank, Ltd.

The Belfast Banking Company, Ltd.

The Ulster Bank, Ltd.

The Northern Banking Company, Ltd.

The Hibernian Bank, Ltd.

The Munster and Leinster Bank, Ltd.
