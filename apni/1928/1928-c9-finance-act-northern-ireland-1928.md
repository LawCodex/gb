# Finance Act (Northern Ireland) 1928

**1928 Chapter 9**

An Act to grant a duty of Excise upon certain licences, and to amend the law relating to certain other duties of Excise and to stamp duty.

[4th June 1928]

*S. 6 rep. by 1948 c. 15 (NI)*

## Supplemental

### 7 Construction and short title.

*Subs. (1) rep. by 1954 c. 33 (NI)*

(2) References in this Act to the Ministry of Finance shall be construed as references to that Ministry acting in the execution of the powers transferred to it either from the Commissioners of Inland Revenue or from the Commissioners of Customs and Excise (as the case may require), and as including references to any officer of a department of the Government of the United Kingdom who is acting in the execution of any such powers on behalf of that Ministry by virtue of an arrangement under section sixty-three[^F1] of the Government of Ireland Act, 1920 [^M1].

(3) This Act may be cited as the Finance Act (Northern Ireland), 1928.
