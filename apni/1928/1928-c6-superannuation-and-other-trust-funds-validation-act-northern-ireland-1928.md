# Superannuation and Other Trust Funds (Validation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1928

**1928 Chapter 6**

An Act to amend the law relating to perpetuities and accumulations, as respects certain benefit funds.

[4th June 1928]

*The Act has been repealed subject to a saving preserving its benefits in relation to funds registered under the Act (see 1975 NI 15 art.65(7))*
