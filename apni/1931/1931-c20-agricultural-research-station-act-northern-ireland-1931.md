# Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland) 1931

**1931 Chapter 20**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 1 Confirmation of agreement and exemption of agreement from stamp duty.

(1) The scheduled agreement is hereby confirmed and shall have effect as if enacted in this Act.

(2) No stamp duty shall be charged on the scheduled agreement.

## 2 Short title, construction and citation.

This Act may be cited as the Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland), 1931, and shall be construed as one with the Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland), 1927 [^M1], and that Act and this Act may be cited together as the Agricultural Research Station Acts (Northern Ireland), 1927 and 1931.
