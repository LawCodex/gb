# Matrimonial Causes Act (Northern Ireland) 1939

**1939 Chapter 13**

An Act to confer jurisdiction upon the [¹High Court] to decree the dissolution of marriages in certain cases; to amend the law relating to matrimonial causes and matters; and for other purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

[4th July 1939]

*The Act has been repealed subject to savings for—*

(a) *petitions for divorce or judicial separation pending at 18.4.1979 (the date on which the repeal of the Act took effect); and*

(b) *proceedings (i) on petitions for damages for adultery, or (ii) for restitution of conjugal rights (including proceedings for certain associated reliefs), pending at 18.4.1979*.

*(see 1978 NI 15 art.63 sch.3)*
