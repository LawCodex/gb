# Indecent Advertisements (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971

**1971 Chapter 18**

An Act to amend the Indecent Advertisements Act 1889.

[8th June 1971]

*S.1 amends s.5 of 1889 c.18*

## 2 Short title and citation.

This Act may be cited as the Indecent Advertisements (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971, and this Act and the Indecent Advertisements Act 1889 [^M1] may be cited together as the Indecent Advertisements Acts (Northern Ireland) 1889 and 1971.

[^M1]: 1889 c.18
