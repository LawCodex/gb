# Midwives and Nursing Homes Act (Northern Ireland) 1929

**1929 Chapter 6 (19 Geo. 5)**

An Act to amend the Midwives (Ireland) Act, 1918, and to provide for the registration and inspection of nursing homes, and for purposes connected therewith.

[16th April 1929]

*This Act has been repealed subject to a saving for the proviso to section 6(3) (registration of nursing homes) which continues to apply to nursing homes and maternity homes in existence before 1st January 1930 (see 1971 c. 32 (NI) s. 22(2))*
