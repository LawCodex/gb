# Allowances to Members of the Assembly (Repeal) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011

**2011 CHAPTER 8**

An Act to repeal the Allowances to Members of the Assembly Act (Northern Ireland) 2000.

[10th February 2011]

BE IT ENACTED by being passed by the Northern Ireland Assembly and assented to by Her Majesty as follows:

## 1 Repeals

(1) The Allowances to Members of the Assembly Act (Northern Ireland) 2000 (c. 3) is repealed.

(2) The entry relating to the Northern Ireland Assembly Commission in Part 1 of the Schedule to the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 (NI 12) is repealed.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into operation on Royal Assent.

## 3 Short title

This Act may be cited as the Allowances to Members of the Assembly (Repeal) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.
