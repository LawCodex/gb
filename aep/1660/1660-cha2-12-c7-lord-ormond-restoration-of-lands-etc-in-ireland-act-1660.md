# Lord Ormond (Restoration of Lands, etc., in Ireland) Act 1660

**1660 CHAPTER 7 12 Cha 2**

[15. An Act for restoreing unto James Marquesse of Ormond all his Honours Mannours Land and Tenements in Ireland whereof he was in possession on the 23th of October 1641 or at any time since.]

[This Act has been called Chap. VII. in the various printed Editions of the Statutes, but it is not entered on the Roll; the Title is entered on the Roll amongst the Titles of Private Acts in the above manner, and is herein inserted in order to keep up the usual arrangementt of Chapters.]
