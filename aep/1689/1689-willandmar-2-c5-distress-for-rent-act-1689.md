# Distress for Rent Act 1689

**1689 c.5**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]I [^F1] Goods distrained for Rent may be appraised and sold, if not replevied with sufficient Security.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]II [^F1] Sheaves or Cocks of Corn loose, &c. or Hay, in any Barn, &c. may be detained, and if not replevied, sold. Corn, &c. not to be removed by Person distraining, to the Damage of Owner, from the Place where seized.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]III [^F1]Pound-breach or Rescous, Treble Damages and Costs.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]IV [^F1]Wrongful Distress, Double Damages and full Costs.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
