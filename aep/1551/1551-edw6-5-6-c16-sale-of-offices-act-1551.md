# Sale of Offices Act 1551

**1551 c. 16**

[^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]For avoiding Corruption in Offices of Justice or Places of Trust;

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]I [^F1] Penalty on selling any Office, or receiving or agreeing to receive Money, &c. for any Office concerning the Administration of Justice, or the Receipt or Controul of the King’s Revenue, or the keeping, &c. of any Town, Castle, or Fortress, or any Clerkship in any Court of Record;On the Seller, Forfeiture of all Title to the Office sold; On the Buyer, Disability to hold such Office bought, &c.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]II [^F1] All such Sales and Agreements, &c, declared void.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]III [^F1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## [^F1]IV [^F1] Acts done by any Officer offending, before his Removal, declared valid.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
