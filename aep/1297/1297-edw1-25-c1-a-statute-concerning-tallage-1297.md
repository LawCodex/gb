# A Statute Concerning Tallage (1297)

**1297 c. 1**

[^X1][^X2]A STATUTE concerning TALLAGE.

## I No Tallage or Aid without Assent of Parliament.

No Tallage or Aid shall be [[^X3]taken] or levied by Us or our Heirs in our Realm, without the good will and Assent of ([^X4]) Archbishops, Bishops, Earls, Barons, Knights, Burgesses, and other [[^X5]Freemen of the Land.]
