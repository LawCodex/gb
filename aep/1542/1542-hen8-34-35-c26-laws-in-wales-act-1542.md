## XLVII Stolen Goods; No Sale in Market shall change the Property; Sale of Cattle out of Market;

Item, That if any goodes or catalles be stollen by any persone or persones and solde in any Faire or Merket within the saide Domynion of Wales, that no suche sale shall change the propertie therof from the owner of the same, but that he maie lawfully cease take and have the same againe upon profe therof made, the said sale notwithstanding. . . .
